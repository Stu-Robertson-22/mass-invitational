Extraction plan

Hire car from Launceston Airport THE PLAN

Collect on landing. About 35 minutes to Lilydale, 20 more to the two vineyards (next door to each other), then about 35 minutes to Scottsdale and 25 to Bridport.

Designated driver RULE

Pick one driver for the day. The driver spits, doesn't sip. Ask for a spittoon at each stop. The other one can buy the bottles.
