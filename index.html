How it works

Rules of combat

Best ball / worst ball match play

  1. Everyone plays their own ball and scores net stableford, off handicap.
  2. Best ball point: on each hole, your pair's best score vs theirs. Higher wins 1 point.
  3. Worst ball point: your pair's worse score vs theirs. Higher wins 1 point.
  4. Tied hole? No point, nothing carries over.
  5. Add up the hole points on each segment. The pair with more wins that segment, and both players bank 1 championship point.

Handicaps

Full handicaps, adjusted daily

  • Everyone plays off their handicap all weekend.
  • Between days, your handicap moves by 50% of the gap between how you played and what your handicap expected.
  • Capped at 2 strokes up or down per day.
e.g. 40 pts = played 4 better than handicap → cut 2 for tomorrow
32 pts = played 4 worse → add 2
Penalty item

The Three-Putt Mascot

  • Three-putt (or worse) and the mascot is yours.
  • Carry it on your bag until someone else three-putts, then hand it over.
  • Counts on every hole, all weekend, bonus holes included.
